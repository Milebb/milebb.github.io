Privacy policy

Zombie Horde · Android
Last updated: 24 November 2026
Developer: Milisav Velenderić

Zombie Horde is a helicopter game. There is no account. This page says what information is involved when you play, watch an ad, buy something, or send a bug report.

On your phone

Progress, settings, and which helicopters or upgrades you own are stored on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use an advertising identifier and similar device data to deliver and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before using data for personalised ads. You can change that choice later in the game, under Settings → Ad privacy.

Buying “No ads”, or the Phantom helicopter, turns off ads between missions. The optional ad that doubles the cash from a mission stays available if you choose to watch it.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your card number.

Bug reports

A bug report is sent only if you tap the button and then send the email yourself. The draft can include the game version, device model, screen size, graphics setting, language, how far you are in the game, and recent error messages. It goes to velenderic.milisav80@gmail.com.

Children

The game is rated Teen on Google Play. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact
